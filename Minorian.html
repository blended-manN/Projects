The Minorian Language

Minorian is a language that I've created as a "burner language." It's basically for when I want characters to speak a language, but I don't want to go through the effort of creating an entirely new language for them. It will mostly be present as the Lingua Franca of Three Dum-Dumz. You can read more about it below.
